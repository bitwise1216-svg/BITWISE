Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we collect only the information you voluntarily provide:

We do not collect any additional personal data. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics by default.

Your information is used solely to:

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

This website uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality (such as remembering your cookie consent preference). We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies unless you have been specifically informed and have consented.

Contact form submissions are processed and stored securely. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

We do not embed third-party social media widgets, advertising scripts, or tracking services.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at bitwise1216@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable laws. Once your inquiry has been resolved and no ongoing business relationship exists, we will delete your data within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
