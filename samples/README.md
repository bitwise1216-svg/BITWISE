# Design & Showcase Samples Folder

This is your dedicated workspace folder to **copy and paste your design samples, photos, mockups, and artwork**.

---

## 📂 Folder Structure

- **`samples/design/`**
  - Paste your **Graphic Design Showcase** samples here.
  - Examples: Brand identity, logos, posters, social media banners, typography, UI designs.
  - *Recommended card aspect ratio for the carousel*: **~360px × 420px** (portrait/editorial) or any high-res image (it auto-fits with cover).

- **`samples/photography/`**
  - Paste your **Photography Showcase** photos here.
  - Examples: Editorial, portrait, landscape, architectural, or studio photos.
  - *Recommended format*: High-res JPG / PNG / WebP.

- **`samples/3d-renders/`**
  - Paste any **3D Showcase** models, renders, or motion stills here.

---

## 🚀 How to Use Your Samples:

### Option 1: Drop them here and tell Antigravity
1. Copy & paste your images into `samples/design/` or `samples/photography/`.
2. Name them however you want (or `design-1.jpg`, `design-2.jpg`, etc.).
3. In chat, simply say: **"I added my samples in the samples folder, please link them to the showcase!"**
4. Antigravity will automatically optimize, link, and display them in the live showcase carousel and gallery!

### Option 2: Live On-Screen Editor (Zero code)
1. Open the website in your browser.
2. Click the floating **"Edit Page"** button in the bottom-right corner.
3. Click on any showcase card (Design 1, Design 2, Photo 1, etc.).
4. Choose your image file directly from your computer to swap it live on screen!
